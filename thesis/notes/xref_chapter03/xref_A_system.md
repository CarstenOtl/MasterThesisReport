## 6. (a) Facts in code/NUTS that the slice omits but a reader needs

1. **Grasp frame is live, not flange-fixed** (5.3). Its linear velocity used by the damper is the mean of the two fingertip velocities, so finger motion enters the damping term (`env.py:1092-1097`).
2. **Decode/clip timing**: target re-decoded and re-clipped at every 120 Hz substep in simulation, at 1 kHz on the robot; the policy only refreshes the raw action at 15 Hz (`controller_reference.md §2`; design doc §9 item 4).
3. **Action-frame noise**: the bolt-tip action frame carries per-reset N(0, 1 mm) noise during training (`env.py:1657`, `yaml:40`).
4. **Damping is on absolute twist** (zero velocity setpoint), and the summed torque is clamped to ±100 N m (`control.py:150-155, 190`); FR3 effort limits 87/12 N m apply afterwards (`gap_todo.md:1205`).
5. **Dead-zone semantics and randomization** (5.11); **nominal contract** when DR is off: EMA 0.0625, zero gain/threshold noise, zero dead zone (`domain_randomization.py:42-100`; `gap_todo.md:1200-1202`). This is the contract under which demonstrations are collected and the student evaluated, and is what the robot reproduces.
6. **Gravity**: arm, nut and bolt have gravity disabled in the threading task (`cfg:67-71, 483-484`); the hardware relies on libfranka gravity compensation (design doc §7). Chapter 5 says this; §3.2.3 does not.
7. **Thread model extras**: bolt–prismatic–carrier–revolute–nut chain; nut–bolt collisions filtered; resistance torque capped at the stopping torque; velocity feed-forward on the prismatic drive; revolute limits ±1440° only to satisfy the schema (`thread_pair.py:168-176`).
8. **Hand drives in sim**: pinch and frozen joints use stiffness 30, effort limit 20 (`cfg:245-246`); inactive-hand stiffness 4.76/damping 0.21 (`cfg:247-248`).
9. **Hardware controller guards** not in sim: torque rate limit 1 N m per 1 ms cycle, `policy_command_timeout 0.5 s`, workspace box, `max_policy_step` guards (`controllers_policy.yaml:85-115`).
10. **Policy inference on hardware**: the workcell PC runs 2 flow-integration steps instead of the 16 trained with, to fit 15 Hz on CPU (`DEP README.md:52-66`). Belongs to §3.5/ch. 5 but affects the "15 Hz" story.
