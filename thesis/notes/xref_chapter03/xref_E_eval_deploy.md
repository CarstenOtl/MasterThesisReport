### 1.2 Does Chapter 4 deliver what §3.6 promises?

- `\ref{sec:evaluation}` (C4 L8), `\ref{sec:ladder}` (C4 L233), `\ref{sec:metrics}` (C5 L27) all resolve; `fig:ladder` is referenced only from §3.6 itself.
- Metric names used in C4 tables match §3.6: "Completed cycles", "cyclic tracking error", "ten-cycle tracking error" (C4 L71, L76–77).
- C4 also reports quantities §3.6 does not define: "maximum turn of 60.3°", "mean absolute retained-turn error 3.1°", "release-time lag 2.7 s / 7.4 s", "controlled-joint RMSE", "TCP position RMSE", "final cumulative nut-turn error" (C4 L122, L142). These come from FU `traj_plot.py` / `evaluation_report.json` (HANDOVER L225–245). Either define them as closed-loop diagnostics in §3.6.2 or label them as such in C4.
- C4 L216 (`tab:online`): "cycles … in one evaluation rollout" and `tab:ladder-results` "one training episode" are single-rollout numbers, which §3.6's "unless a study states otherwise" covers.
- Ladder: C4 uses rung 1 (offline fit) and rung 3 only (see E1).
